About Velkar Polychem

Materials that move manufacturing forward.

Velkar Polychem, established in 2012 and based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, supplies engineering plastics, polymer raw materials and industrial plastic products to manufacturers, moulders, processors and industrial buyers.

Our focus is dependable sourcing, responsive B2B support and helping customers identify suitable materials for their applications.
